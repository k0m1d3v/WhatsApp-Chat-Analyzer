Your chat, wrapped.

Stats, awards and charts for any WhatsApp chat: who talks the most, who replies fastest, when you chat, your top emojis.

🔒 Everything runs in your browser. Your chat is never uploaded anywhere.

How do I export a chat?
  1. Open the chat (or group) in WhatsApp.
  2. iPhone: tap the name at the top → Export Chat. Android: ⋮ → More → Export chat.
  3. Choose Without media and save or send the file to yourself.
  4. Drop the .zip (or the .txt inside it) above.
✨ Add an AI recap (optional, uses your own API key)

Your key is only used to call the provider directly from this page; it's never sent anywhere else. Only the most recent ~150,000 characters of the chat are sent. Make sure the people in the chat would be OK with it.
